Beyond looking bad, mold endangers health and tells you there is a moisture problem underneath, one that can weaken the building itself. Mold exposure can cause respiratory issues, allergic reactions, headaches and more severe health effects, particularly in children, the elderly and people with compromised immune systems. At North Richland Hills Restoration Co, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in North Richland Hills homes and commercial properties.

Wiping away the mold you can see is not remediation. Professional remediation means identifying the moisture source feeding the mold, setting up containment to prevent cross-contamination, removing affected materials safely with HEPA-filtered equipment, treating the remaining surfaces with antimicrobial solutions and fixing the underlying moisture problem so it does not recur. The result of that thorough process is mold dealt with at its source, never simply masked.

Each project starts with a careful inspection, and in many cases we test the air to learn which mold spores are present and at what concentration. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. While the work is underway, the area stays under negative air pressure, HEPA scrubbers clean the air and our crew follows IICRC S520, the mold remediation standard. A final verification step, which can include a clearance air test on request, shows that the space meets the standard for safe occupancy.

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Use ventilation and a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60%, with 30 to 50% as the ideal
  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Fuzzy or spotted growth on stored boxes, clothing or the backs of furniture
  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • Signs of concealed moisture such as paint that bubbles, wallpaper that peels or materials that warp
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • Persistent musty or earthy odors, especially in enclosed or humid areas
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house

What a Professional Brings to Mold Remediation

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.
